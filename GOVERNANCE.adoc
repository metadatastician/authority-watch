= Governance

== Safety authority

The source publisher controls what was published. Authority Watch records that
publication. A qualified human reviewer determines an interpretation. A
separate approval event authorises a rule-pack change. Signing attests to the
approved bundle; it does not create legal truth.

== Review

Production legal-rule changes require two identified reviewers. During private
solo experimentation, one curator may produce only a bundle marked
`PROVISIONAL_SINGLE_REVIEW`. Such a bundle cannot support an accredited or
professionally validated assessment claim.

Reviews and corrections are append-only events. A correction supersedes but
does not erase its predecessor.

== Publication

Unsigned, revoked, stale, rollback-unsafe, or unapproved bundles must not
activate. Operators remain responsible for professional and legal review.

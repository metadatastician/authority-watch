= Temporal semantics

Publication, making, laying, enactment, royal assent, commencement,
effective-from, effective-until, revision publication, guidance issue and
withdrawal, consultation open and close, judgment, appeal status, observation,
review, approval and release are separate facts.

Missing effective dates remain missing. They are never inferred from
publication dates.

= Impact graph

The graph links source, authority provision, interpreted rule, obligation or
permission, procedure, scenario action, assessment criterion, golden run and
certificate claim.

An unmapped authority change is a coverage gap, not evidence of no impact.
Traversal may yield documentation-only, rule/scenario/rubric review, invalid
golden runs, certificate wording review, assessment blocking, historical
validity, or urgent withdrawal.

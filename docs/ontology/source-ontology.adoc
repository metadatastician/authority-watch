= Source ontology

Grades describe the role a source may play; they do not remove the need for
interpretation.

* A: enacted or made primary/formal authority and properly interpreted binding
  judgments.
* B: formal professional or regulatory requirements.
* C: official guidance and operational requirements.
* D: prospective Bills, drafts, consultations and pending legal events.
* E: secondary commentary useful for issue discovery only.

Every source identity includes publisher, class, grade, jurisdiction, coverage,
official and machine URIs, licence, terms review time, retrieval method,
cadence, review owner and enabled state.

= Authority change ontology

An `AuthorityChangeCandidate` joins one or more observations with byte,
structural and candidate-semantic diffs. It records authority grade,
jurisdiction, provisions, publication and legal status, distinct dates,
machine classification and confidence, human classification, possible
downstream identifiers, review state, discussion and signatures.

The word `candidate` is load-bearing: no machine-produced classification is a
final legal interpretation.

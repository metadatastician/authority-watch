= UK insolvency profile

This profile declares an initial monitoring scope. It contains no approved
legal interpretation and all adapters are disabled until their terms,
licensing, retrieval behaviour and fixtures are reviewed.

Initial formal legislation includes the Insolvency Act 1986, Company Directors
Disqualification Act 1986, Insolvency (England and Wales) Rules 2016, and
Corporate Insolvency and Governance Act 2020.

Professional and assessment sources include issued and proposed Statements of
Insolvency Practice, professional codes and notices, and JIEB syllabus and
candidate material. Syllabus changes are distinct from changes in law.

Find Case Law remains disabled. Manual judgment metadata may be ingested only
with an official origin while API terms, computational-analysis status,
licensing, attribution and retention obligations remain under review.

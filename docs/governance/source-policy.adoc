= Source policy

Every source declares its publisher, class, authority grade, jurisdiction,
coverage, official and machine URIs, licence, terms review date, retrieval
method, expected cadence, review owner and enabled state.

Adapters prefer official APIs and feeds, identify themselves, make conditional
requests, obey documented limits, cache, back off, preserve attribution and
never bypass access controls. Monitoring a declared URL does not grant a
licence to redistribute its content.

Grade E commentary may surface an issue for human investigation but cannot
independently support an authoritative rule.

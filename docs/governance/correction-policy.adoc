= Correction and revocation policy

Observations and reviews are never destructively rewritten. A correction cites
the affected identifiers, explains the error, supplies replacement evidence
and records its reviewer and signature.

Revocation prevents activation and creates a signed revocation record. It does
not erase historical evidence. Consumers retain the prior pack only for
explicit historical replay with visible warnings.

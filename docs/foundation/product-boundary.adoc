= Product boundary

Authority Watch detects and preserves evidence about publications. It assists
humans in classification and downstream impact review. It does not provide
legal advice, determine legal effect autonomously, accredit assessments, or
install production updates without review.

A changed byte is evidence of a changed representation, not necessarily a
legal change. A machine classification is a suggestion, not an interpretation.
A signature authenticates bytes and an approval history; it does not certify
that an interpretation is professionally correct.

= Human governance

Production legal-rule changes require two reviewers with recorded identities,
roles, scope, inspected evidence, interpretation, effective-date reasoning,
uncertainties, decision, time and signature.

The solo development profile permits one provisional curator. Its output is
always marked `PROVISIONAL_SINGLE_REVIEW`, cannot become
`CURRENT_VERIFIED`, and cannot support formal certification.

Specialist uncertainty is a first-class outcome. `NeedsSpecialistAdvice`,
`AwaitingCommencement`, `AwaitingAppeal`, `AwaitingExternalClarification` and
`SourceDisputed` preserve uncertainty rather than forcing a conclusion.

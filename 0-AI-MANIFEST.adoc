= AI Use Manifest — Authority Watch
:toc:
:icons: font

[.lead]
This document is the *universal entry point* for AI agents working in this
repository. It defines the boundaries, constraints, and expectations for
machine assistance in the _authority-watch_ project.

== Overview

Authority Watch is a high-assurance legal/regulatory change monitoring system.
AI agents may assist with analysis, classification, and documentation, but
*human review and approval are always required* for any authoritative determination.

== What AI May Do

AI assistance is permitted for:

* Identifying changed clauses in legal documents
* Proposing classifications for detected changes
* Summarising diffs between document versions
* Suggesting impact links between changes
* Proposing test cases for review state transitions
* Generating documentation and examples
* Suggesting code improvements and refactorings

== What AI May NOT Do

AI assistance is *prohibited* from:

* Determining legal effect conclusively
* Approving any change or rule pack
* Choosing an unsupported effective date
* Marking any rule pack as "current"
* Signing any release or bundle
* Transforming observed material directly into an authoritative rule
* Bypassing any review state transition (see `ReviewState` in code)
* Activating any bundle without explicit human approval
* Modifying production observations
* Committing private review material, signing keys, or credentials

== Requirements for Retained AI Suggestions

Every machine suggestion that is retained *must* record:

. The model or tool identity (e.g., "Mistral Vibe", "Claude Code")
. The prompt or operation that generated it
. The input observation identifiers (e.g., SHA-256 digest of source)
. The output digest (SHA-256 of the generated content)
. The timestamp of generation
. The human disposition (accepted/modified/rejected)

== System Independence

The system *must* remain fully operable without external AI assistance.
No production workflow may depend on AI availability.

== Safety Principles

. *Observation is not interpretation.* A document may be observed without being understood.
. *Interpretation is not approval.* A document may be understood without being approved.
. *Approval is not publication.* An approved change may remain unpublished.
. *Publication is not truth.* A published rule is authoritative only by virtue of its source, not by being in this system.

== Agent Protocol

. Read this file first
. Consult `AGENTS.md` for project-specific rules
. Check `.machine_readable/ai/` for additional constraints
. Verify all suggestions against human review requirements
. Never claim a machine-generated result is authoritative

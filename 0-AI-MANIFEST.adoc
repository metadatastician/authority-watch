= AI Use Manifest

Machine assistance may identify changed clauses, propose classifications,
summarise diffs, suggest impact links, and propose tests.

Machine assistance may not determine legal effect conclusively, approve a
change, choose an unsupported effective date, mark a rule pack current, sign a
release, or transform observed material directly into an authoritative rule.

Every retained machine suggestion must record the model or tool identity,
prompt or operation, input observation identifiers, output digest, timestamp,
and human disposition. The system must remain operable without external AI.
